In the app

  1. Sign in with your e-mail and the activation code. The account owner's code came by e-mail when the company connected — check spam, or ask us for a new one.
  2. Branding — your company's name, logo and colors (owners and admins).
  3. Your catalog — GWP's shared catalog in your Jobber, and your prices.
  4. Manage Team — lists your Jobber users; give each the access they need (owners and admins).
  5. Your trial or subscription — the strip at the top of the app shows where it stands.

Billing and account questions (owners): support@golfwellplayed.com

Disconnecting from Jobber

In Jobber, open Apps, find Golf Sim Plan Generator and choose Disconnect. Then:

  • The app stops working with your Jobber — no quotes sent, no products read. Its Jobber actions show as disconnected.
  • Nothing is deleted. Your plans, catalog settings and team stay as they are.
  • Jobber archives the app's GWP Catalog custom field while the app is disconnected; it comes back with its values when you reconnect.
  • Reconnect from Jobber's App Marketplace at any time and carry on where you left off.
  • Disconnecting doesn't cancel a subscription. To close your account, e-mail us; its data is then deleted as the Privacy Policy describes.
