What it does

Built by Golf Well Played for golf simulator installers who run their business on Jobber. Two things Jobber doesn't do on its own:

1. Products that know their specs

Extends each Jobber product or service with the attributes a room plan needs — a projector's throw and brightness, an enclosure's size, a mat's footprint — and with packages and bundles that group them the way you sell them.

2. Quotes created for you

From the plan, the quote is created in Jobber with the right products, packages and bundles. Finalize it in Jobber as you always do; the app reads it back to keep the plan and the quote in step.

3. Documents from the quote

Contractor-ready install sheets for the customer's contractors and your crew. A customer-facing project summary, generated from the plan and the quote your customer approved.

And everything around it

Install sheets

Floor plan, enclosure, ceiling blocking, wiring, screen and turf — every measurement checked against the room, ready for your crew.

3D view and project summary

A 3D view of the room and a printable project summary to walk your customer through the design.

Connected to Jobber

Load a client and their quote, and plan with your own Jobber products and prices — Jobber stays your system for clients, quotes and jobs.

A shared product catalog

Start with GWP's catalog of projectors, launch monitors, mats, turf and enclosures — with the specs the plans need — added to your Jobber and kept up to date.

Your team, your brand

Your company's name, logo and colors on every sheet; your reps and project managers with the access their Jobber role gives them.

Works on site

A desktop app for Windows and Mac that keeps working without a signal at the customer's site, and updates itself.

Get started

Every company starts with a free trial.

  1. Connect from Jobber. Add the GWP Sim Plan Generator to your Jobber account (the account owner approves it). Your company is set up and the owner receives an activation code by e-mail.
  2. Download the app for Windows or Mac (below) and sign in with that code.
  3. Add your brand — your company's name, logo and colors.
  4. Set up your catalog — add GWP's shared catalog to your Jobber, then set your prices.
  5. Add your team — your Jobber users appear in Manage Team; give each the access they need.
  6. Plan a room — start a New Plan at your next site visit.

Download the app

Install once — after that the app updates itself.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11. Unzip and run the installer.

Mac — Apple silicon

M1, M2 and later. Unzip, open the .dmg and drag the app into Applications.

Mac — Intel

Older Macs with an Intel chip. Unzip, open the .dmg and drag the app into Applications.

Which Mac? Apple menu → About This Mac → Chip.
On Windows, if it says "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway. On a Mac, after moving the app to Applications, open Terminal once and run xattr -r -d com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/GWP Sim Plan Generator.app"
