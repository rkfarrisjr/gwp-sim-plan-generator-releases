Data Processing Addendum — GWP (Golf) Sim Plan Generator

Last modified: Nov 12, 2026, 12:25 PM ET (UTC−5)

1. Roles and scope

This Addendum is between Golf Well Played, LLC (“GWP”, “we”, “us”) and the Company named in the Terms of Service. It applies when GWP processes personal information about the Company’s clients and prospects, or products (“Client Data”) in providing the Service. For Client Data, the Company decides why and how it is processed (the controller, or “business”); GWP processes it only on the Company’s behalf (the processor, or “service provider”). Information about the Company’s own users is covered by GWP’s Privacy Policy instead.

3. GWP’s obligations

GWP will:

  1. Process Client Data only to provide the Service and on the Company’s documented instructions (the Terms, this Addendum, and the Company’s use of the Service), and never sell or share it for advertising.
  2. Make sure its staff and contractors with access are bound to confidentiality.
  3. Keep appropriate security measures, including encryption of Client Data in transit and at rest on GWP’s systems, separation of each company’s data, and role-based access.
  4. Notify the Company without undue delay, and within 72 hours of becoming aware, of a breach affecting its Client Data, with the information the Company needs to meet its own obligations.
  5. Help the Company, as far as reasonable, to answer requests from its clients to access, correct or delete their information.
  6. Tell the Company if it believes an instruction breaks applicable law.

4. Subprocessors

The Company authorizes GWP to use the subprocessors listed in the Privacy Policy (i.e., Xano, Jobber or other integrated system as connected by the Company, Hookdeck, Resend, and Stripe once payment starts). GWP holds each to data-protection terms at least as protective as this Addendum, stays responsible for them, and gives at least 30 days’ notice of a new subprocessor. The Company may object on reasonable grounds; if it can’t be resolved, the Company may close its account.

5. Plan sharing, deletion, audits and precedence

  • Plan sharing is the Company’s instruction. When a Company user sends a lead share, GWP transfers the client’s name and contact details to the receiving company on the Company’s instruction. From then on, the receiving company is responsible for its copy.
  • Deletion. After the account closes, GWP deletes Client Data within 180 days, unless the law requires keeping it. Copies in backups are overwritten on their normal cycle.
  • The Company’s devices. Client Data on the Company’s own devices (plans saved in the desktop app, exported PDFs, printouts) is outside GWP’s systems. Securing those devices, including encryption on the device, is the Company’s responsibility.
  • Audits. On reasonable request, no more than once a year, GWP will answer a security questionnaire and provide information needed to show it meets this Addendum.
  • Precedence. If this Addendum and the Terms conflict on Client Data, this Addendum wins.
