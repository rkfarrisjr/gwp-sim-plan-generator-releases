Privacy Policy — The GWP (Golf) Sim Plan Generator

Last modified: Nov 7, 2026, 4:53 PM ET (UTC−5)

1. Who we are and whose information this covers

Golf Well Played, LLC (860 Peachtree Street NE, Suite 2205, Atlanta, GA 30308; “GWP”, “we”, “us”) runs the GWP (Golf) “Sim Plan Generator” (the “Service”). This policy covers personal information about the people who use the Service on behalf of a company: owners, admins and sales reps (“users”).

The Service also holds information about the companies’ own clients (for example, a homeowner’s name and address on a plan). For that information, the company decides how it is used and GWP acts on its behalf under our Data Processing Addendum. Clients with questions about it should contact the company they are working with.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the Service: sign you in, show your company’s catalog and plans, write quotes and products to your company’s Jobber account, and deliver shares you accept.
  • To send service e-mails and texts: activation codes, welcome messages, share notices, and account or billing notices.
  • To keep the Service secure and working: monitor errors and usage limits, prevent misuse, and keep each company’s data separate.
  • To provide contractors and subcontractors information they need to deliver their services to you.
  • To improve the Service, using aggregated information that doesn’t identify you.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don’t sell personal information, and we don’t use it for advertising.

5. Security

  • Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Personal information is encrypted in transit and at rest on our servers, with keys held only by our servers.
  • Every request is checked against the signed-in user’s company, so one company can’t reach another’s data.
  • Access is limited by role: only owners and admins manage users, sharing agreements and the catalog.
  • We monitor errors and usage limits and are alerted when something looks wrong.
  • Your devices are yours to secure. Copies on your company’s own devices, such as plans saved in the desktop app, exported PDFs and printouts, are under your company’s control. Protecting those devices (for example, disk encryption, screen locks and access to shared computers) is your company’s responsibility.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we’ll tell you as the law requires.

  • Access, correction and deletion. You can ask us for a copy of your personal information, to correct it, or to delete it, at privacy@golfwellplayed.com. Some requests go through your company, since it controls your account. Depending on where you live, you may have further rights under state law; we honor them as the law requires and won’t treat you differently for using them.
  • Children. The Service is for businesses and not meant for anyone under 18.
  • Where data is stored. In the United States (per Xano’s agreement with GWP).
  • Changes. We’ll post updates here with a new last “Modified date”, and tell users by e-mail or in the app’s release notes about any material changes.
