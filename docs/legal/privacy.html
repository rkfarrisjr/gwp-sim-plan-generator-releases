Privacy Policy — The GWP (Golf) Sim Plan Generator

Last modified: Nov 12, 2026, 12:25 PM ET (UTC−5)

1. Who we are and whose information this covers

Golf Well Played, LLC (1007 Mansell Rd., Suite A-172, Roswell, GA 30076; “GWP”, “we”, “us”) runs the GWP (Golf) “Sim Plan Generator” (the “Service”). This policy covers personal information about the people who use the Service on behalf of a company: owners, admins and sales reps (“users”).

The Service also holds information about the companies’ own clients (for example, a homeowner’s name and address on a plan). For that information, the company decides how it is used and GWP acts on its behalf under our Data Processing Addendum. Clients with questions about it should contact the company they are working with.

3. How we use it

  • To provide the Service: sign you in, show your company’s catalog and plans, write quotes and products to your company’s Jobber account, and deliver Sim plan shares between users of the Service.
  • To send service e-mails and texts: activation codes, welcome messages, share notices, and account or billing notices.
  • To keep the Service secure and working: monitor errors and usage limits, prevent misuse, and keep each company’s data separate.
  • To provide contractors and subcontractors information they need to deliver their services to you.
  • To improve the Service, using aggregated information that doesn’t identify you.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We don’t sell personal information to 3rd Parties.

5. Security

  • Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Personal information is encrypted in transit and at rest on our servers, with keys held within the Service.
  • Every request is checked against the signed-in user’s company, so one company can’t reach another’s data.
  • Access is limited by role: only owners and admins manage users, sharing agreements and the product catalog with its associated attributes per product type.
  • We monitor errors and usage limits and are alerted when something looks wrong.
  • Your devices are yours to secure. Copies on your company’s own devices, such as plans saved in the desktop app, exported PDFs and printouts, are under your company’s control. Protecting those devices, printouts and digital assets (for example, disk encryption, screen locks and access to shared computers) is your company’s responsibility.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we’ll notify you as the law requires.

Some requests go through your company, since it controls your account.   • Changes. We’ll post updates here with a new “last modified date”, and tell users by e-mail or in the release notes - available within the Sim Plan Generator app - about any material changes.
