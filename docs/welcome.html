Download the app

1. Download the app

Windows

Unzip and run the installer.

Mac — Apple silicon

M1, M2 and later.

Mac — Intel

Older Macs with an Intel chip.

Windows: if it says "Windows protected your PC", choose More info, then Run anyway. Mac: drag the app into Applications, then open Terminal once and run xattr -r -d com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/GWP Sim Plan Generator.app"

  1. Sign in with your e-mail and the activation code.
  2. Branding — your company's name, logo and colors.

  4. Your team — Manage Team lists your Jobber users; give each the access they need.
  5. Your trial — the strip at the top shows the days left.
